Prometheus Bound

This is the 1851 American edition of this book.  It includes Browning's retranslation of Aeschylus' play Prometheus Bound, "Sonnets from the Portuguese", "Casa Guidi Windows", and a selection of sonnets and other poems.

For the 1833 edition of this book, see Prometheus Bound (Browning, 1833).
