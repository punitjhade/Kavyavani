Dictionary of National Biography, 1885-1900 (1885)

→

standard work of reference with articles on more than 29,000 notable figures from British history. It was originally published in 63 volumes between 1885 and 1900, by Smith, Elder & Co.

The project is fairly complete. All the DNB articles are transcribed, but not all are fully proofread. Each article links to scanned images of the original published pages to allow a reader to verify the text.
