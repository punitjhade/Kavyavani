​Tennyson, Alfred, Baron Tennyson; created D.C.L. June, 1855, poet laureate 1850 (3s. George Clayton Tennyson, rector of Somersby, co. Lincoln), hon. fellow Trinity Coll., Cambridge, F.R.S., created a peer 24 Jan., 1884. See Foster's Our Noble and Gentle Families.
