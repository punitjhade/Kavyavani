The Dunciad (1743)
by Alexander Pope

→

'The Dunciad' first appeared in print in 1728, as a poem of some 920 lines in three books. The first 'Variorum' edition, with notes and elements of the 'critical apparatus', appeared in 1729, the poem having grown to 1014 lines, still in three books. In 1742 appeared the 'New Dunciad', a work of 618 lines which subsequently became Book IV. It was not until October 1743 that a complete edition of the four books and the associated 'critical apparatus' appeared; this is a transcription of that edition. [Based on Alexander Pope: A Bibliography Volume 1, Part 1 (1922), and Alexander Pope: A Bibliography Volume 1, Part 2 (1927), both by Reginald Harvey Griffith]
