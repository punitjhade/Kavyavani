This pamphlet is an edition from 1793 of the earliest work by Pope to appear in print. It originally featured in Poetical Miscellanies: The Sixth Part. Containing a Collection of Original Poems, With Several New Translations. By the most Eminent Hands. (1709), pp.721-751. [Based on Alexander Pope: A Bibliography, Volume 1, Part 1 (1922), by Reginald Harvey Griffith.]
