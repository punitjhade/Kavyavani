​PARLIAMENT OF FOWLS, The, or The
Assembly of Fowls. One of Chaucer's minor
poems, written in his favorite form of a
vision. A student reading the “Dream of Scipio”
in Cicero's Republic falls asleep, and is led by
Scipio into a garden where nature assembles
the birds on Saint Valentine's Day, and holds
a court of love. The poem is taken partly
from Boccaccio's Tescide and alludes to other
Italian sources, but is based mainly on Macrobius's
Commentary on Scipio's Dream, a favorite
book in the Middle Ages.
