Published in 1385, Troilus and Criseyde is Geoffrey Chaucer's poem in rhyme royal (rime royale) re-telling the tragic love story of Troilus, a Trojan prince, and Criseyde. Many Chaucer scholars regard this as his best work, even including the better known but incomplete Canterbury Tales."Troilus and Criseyde," in Wikipedia, the Free Encyclopedia

See also Shakespeare's later rendition, at Troilus and Cressida (Shakespeare)
