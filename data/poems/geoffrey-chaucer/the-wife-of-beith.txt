This poem features a character derived from Geoffrey Chaucer's Wife of Bath in the Canterbury Tales, but this work was not written by him.
Of the 16 versions listed below, all are chapbooks from the National Library of Scotland. Most of them carry no publication date and some do not have an identified printer. There are some duplicates, which are identified below (see also Discussion page).
The number in square brackets corresponds to the 'suffix' used on the Index pages.
