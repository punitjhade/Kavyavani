​Arnold, Matthew, 1s. Thomas, of Laleham, Middlesex, D.D. Balliol Coll., matric. 28 Nov., 1840, aged 17, B.A. 1844, fellow Oriel Coll., M.A. 1853, created D.C.L. 21 June, 1870, professor of poetry, 1857-67.
