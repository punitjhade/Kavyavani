First published in 1687, this transcription is of the second edition, which was issued the same year. It corrects a number of errata in the first edition, and introduces a few minor textual changes. [Based on John Dryden: A Bibliography of Early Editions and of Drydeniana (1936), by Hugh Macdonald, pp.44-47.]
