MARRIAGE
A-la-Mode.

A

COMEDY.

As it is Acted at the

THEATRE-ROYAL.

Written by JOHN DRYDEN, Servant to His Majesty.

————————Quicquid sum ego, quamvis
Infra Lucilli censum ingeniumque, tamen me
Cum magnis vixisse, invita fatebitur usque
Invidia, & fragili quærens illidere dentem
Offendet solido.
Horat. Serm.

LONDON,

Printed by T. N. for Henry Herringman, and are to be
sold at the Anchor in the Lower Walk of
the New Exchange. 1673.
