Aur. Heavens! Can you this without just Vengeance bear?

Aur.When will you Thunder, if you now are clear?

Aur.Yet her alone let not your Thunder seize,

Aur.I too deserve to dye, because I please.

Hip. Magne Regnator deum

Hip.Tam lentus audis scelera tam lentus vides

Hip.Ecquando sæva fulmen emittes Manu

Hip.Si nunc serenum est

Hip.————— Me velox cremet.

Hip.Transactus ignis sum Nocens; merui mori

Hip.Placui Novercæ.

Hipp. ————Thesei vultus amo

Hipp.Illos priores, ques tulit quondam puer

Hipp.Cum prima turas signare barba Genas.

Hipp.I am not chang'd, I love my Husband still,

Hipp.But love him as he was when youthful Grace,

Hipp.And the first Bloom began to shade his Face.

Dal. I see thou art implacable, more Deaf

Dal.To Prayers, than Winds and Seas; yet Winds to Seas

Dal.Are reconcil'd at length, and Sea to Shoar

Dal.Thy anger unappeaseable still rages;

Dal.Eternal Tempest never to be calm'd.

Eng. Unmov'd he stood, and deaf to all my Prayers,

Eng.As Seas and Winds to sinking Mariners:

Eng.But Seas grow calm, and Winds are reconcil'd;

Eng.Her Tyrant Beauty never grows more mild.
