​Dodgson, Rev. Charles Lutwidge, 1s. Charles, of Daresbury, Cheshire, cler. Christ Church, matric. 23 May, 1850, aged 18; student 1852-70, B.A. 1854, M.A. 1857, a senior student 1858, mathematical lecturer 1855-81.
