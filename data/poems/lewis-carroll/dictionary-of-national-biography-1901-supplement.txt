The 1901 Supplement contains three volumes.

Shortcut:
DNB01

Background: The 1901 Supplement to the Dictionary of National Biography, 1885-1900, covers subjects who had died between 1885 and 1900 or were otherwise excluded from the original publication. The supplements added notable figures up to the death of Queen Victoria on 22 January 1901. Corrections (Errata) were then added, initially in 1904.
