The Famous
TRAGEDY
OF
THE RICH IEVV
OF MALTA.

AS IT VVAS PLAYD
BEFORE THE King AND
Qveene, IN HIS MAJESTIES
Theatre at White-Hall, by her Majesties
Servants at the Cock-pit.

Written by Christopher Marlo.

LONDON;

Printed by I. B. for Nicholas Vavasour, and are to be sold at his Shop in the Inner-Temple, neere the Church. 1633.
